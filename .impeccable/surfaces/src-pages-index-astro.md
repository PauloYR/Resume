# Portfólio (todas as páginas)

Scope and mode: Experience (portfolio). Single static site, pt-BR, mobile-first, dark and light themes.
Audience, job, action: recruiters and tech leads on phones; they must understand who Paulo is and contact him or download the resume. Proof: resume metrics only (no invented clients, no invented testimonials).
Constraints: purple and blue, no phone number in HTML, no third-party CDNs, WCAG AA in both themes, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. The user chose the category standard near the Figma template after previewing; the earlier lace/thread world is discarded.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard played straight at full craft: a clean card-based developer portfolio in the template's structure, with purple and blue replacing green. Refuses decoration that does not carry information.

OWN-WORLD: dark theme is a graphite-violet ground with raised surfaces and 1px borders; light theme is a pale lavender ground with white surfaces. Accents: violet and blue, with the gradient used only on the headline highlight word and the primary button. Display type is a confident grotesk, text type a calm humanist sans, monospace only for code and numbers. Cards are 14px-radius surfaces with a single border and no shadow stack.

STORY: the visitor reads who Paulo is, sees measured outcomes, and acts through one clear primary button (Falar comigo) with the resume PDF one tap away.

FIRST VIEWPORT: headline "Olá, sou o Paulo, engenheiro backend em Go" with the highlight phrase in the brand gradient, a one-line positioning statement, primary button and a quieter resume link, then a three-part bio row (biografia, o que uso, disponibilidade) peeking below on phones.

FORM: category standard (canon), chosen by the user; seed key 6225f659. Signature details: focus ring and hover lift on cards, smooth theme switch, company marquee as the one moving element, GitHub contribution panel in the brand scale.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
